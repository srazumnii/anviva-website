Privacy Policy

Operator: Anviva · Site: anviva.ru · Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Policy describes what data is processed when you use the Google Workspace™ Editor Add-on Anviva DocGen (the “Service”) and related pages on anviva.ru. It is intended for Google Workspace™ Marketplace users and OAuth access involving Google APIs.

Limited Use of Google API data is described on the Limited Use page.

1. Operator

The Service is operated by Anviva. For privacy and support inquiries, email support@anviva.ru (anviva.ru domain).

2. Access

Via OAuth / Apps Script the Service can access only what the add-on needs:

  • The current Google Sheets™ spreadsheet and current Google Docs™ document where the add-on is open.
  • Google Drive™ files the add-on created (template copies, generated documents, PDFs, images uploaded from the sidebar) — drive.file scope. This is not access to your entire Drive.
  • The email address of the Google account you are signed in with.
  • Support request contents that you submit via the “Support” and “Request license” forms.

We do not use Google Picker and we do not read arbitrary Drive files from a pasted ID unless the file was created by the add-on or is the current Sheet/Doc.

3. Use

Data is used only to provide add-on features:

  • reading registries, directories, and parameters in your spreadsheet;
  • applying masks in a Docs template and creating the finished document / PDF next to the template;
  • saving a template from the current Docs file (“Save template”);
  • uploading images from the add-on sidebar;
  • checking trial and license status;
  • sending your support or license request — only when you submit the form.

Google Workspace™ data is not used to train AI/ML models and is not sold. See Limited Use.

4. Transfer

We do not sell personal data. Sharing is limited to:

  • Control (our license-registry Web App): installation and license metadata (spreadsheet id, trial/license state, dates) — not the contents of your documents.
  • Support: messages you send from the add-on go to support@anviva.ru.
  • Google infrastructure (Apps Script / Google APIs) within the permissions you grant.
  • Where required by law.

On Drive the Service works with files the add-on created and with the current Sheet/Doc — not “entire Drive”.

5. Protect

Processing runs through Google Apps Script on your behalf within OAuth. We rely on Google Workspace™ security controls, limit access to the support inbox, and do not request restricted Drive. Absolute security on the internet cannot be guaranteed; if you suspect an incident, email support@anviva.ru.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Spreadsheets, templates, and generated files remain in your Google Drive™ / Google Workspace™; the Service does not take them off-platform beyond the current operation.
  • License metadata in Control — for the license term plus a reasonable period afterward (accounting, disputes).
  • Support inquiries — typically up to 24 months, unless law requires otherwise.

You may:

  • revoke add-on access in Google permission settings;
  • uninstall the add-on and delete files in your Drive;
  • ask us to delete support messages and license metadata by emailing support@anviva.ru with the subject “Anviva DocGen / personal data”.

7. Your rights

You may request information about data the operator holds in connection with support or licensing, and ask to correct inaccuracies where this does not conflict with lawful retention grounds.

8. Children

The Service is intended for business use in Google Workspace™ and is not directed at children under 16.

9. Policy changes

The current version is always available at https://anviva.ru/privacy-en.html (Russian: https://anviva.ru/privacy.html). The last-updated date is shown at the top of this page.
