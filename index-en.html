Anviva DocGen

Documents from spreadsheets — without copy-paste

Anviva DocGen is a Google Workspace Editor Add-on for Google Sheets and Google Docs. It generates business documents (contracts, acts, invoices, and similar files) from spreadsheet data and Docs templates using field masks, registers, and licensing.

About the app Support

What Anviva DocGen does

Clear purpose for users and for Google API review — no login required to read this page.

Anviva DocGen helps teams create documents from data they already keep in Google Sheets™, using templates in Google Docs™. Generated files are saved to the user’s Google Drive™. The add-on is used for ordinary business document workflows (for example contracts, acts, and invoices), not for generating or distributing intimate imagery.

Anviva DocGen does not use Google APIs for AI-generated Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (AI NCII). The add-on does not create, process, or distribute such content.

Use of Google user data follows the Privacy Policy and Limited Use disclosures. See also the Terms of Service.

How it works

Three steps from spreadsheet data to a finished Drive file.

  1. Step 01

    Data in Sheets

    Keep registers and parameters in a Google Sheet — your usual working surface.

  2. Step 02

    Template in Docs

    Place field masks in a Google Docs template — Anviva DocGen fills them at generation time.

  3. Step 03

    Finished document

    Create a contract or act with one command; the file appears in your Google Drive.

Who it is for

Teams already on Google Workspace who assemble the same document types repeatedly.

Legal, accounting, sales, and operations teams — anywhere the same spreadsheet fields regularly become standard contracts, acts, invoices, and attachments.

The product UI is available in Russian; this English page describes the same Anviva DocGen add-on for reviewers and international users.

Features

What you need for day-to-day document generation.

  • Masks in templates Substitute fields, directories, and table blocks in Google Docs.
  • Registers in Sheets Your spreadsheet is the source of truth; generation runs on selected rows.
  • Licensing Trial period and license activation from the add-on sidebar.
  • In-product support “Support” and “Request license” forms in the Sheets/Docs sidebar.
