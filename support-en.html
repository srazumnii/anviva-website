Support

Help with installation, licensing, templates, and Google API access.

How to contact us

Email is the primary support channel for Anviva DocGen.

support@anviva.ru

Please include: organization name, spreadsheet/document (link if possible), and a short description of the steps where the issue occurred.

From the add-on UI

In the Anviva DocGen sidebar in Google Sheets or Google Docs you can use:

  • “Support” — a message to the operator with request context;
  • “Request license” — a request to activate or renew a license.

Requests go to the same support address. How we process data is described on the Privacy Policy page.

FAQ

After an add-on update, Google asks me to grant access again
This can happen when the set of scopes (Google permissions) expands. Open the add-on and confirm the new permissions in the Google dialog. You can review existing grants in account settings.
How does the trial work?
After installation a trial period is available; license status is shown in the sidebar. To activate a full license, use “Request license” or email us.
Where are generated documents stored?
In your Google Drive (or your Workspace organization’s Drive). Anviva DocGen creates files on your behalf via Apps Script; we do not move finished documents to a third-party drive.
Where is the Privacy Policy?
https://anviva.ru/privacy-en.html — use this URL in OAuth / Marketplace fields. Russian version: https://anviva.ru/privacy.html.
