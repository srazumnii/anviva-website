Limited Use

Anviva DocGen · Last updated: September 10, 2026

Anviva DocGen’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell user data

Anviva does not sell Google Workspace™ user data and does not transfer it for advertising or independent marketing profiles.

No AI / ML training on Workspace data

Google Workspace™ data (Google Sheets™, Google Docs™, Google Drive™ files, and other content the add-on can access) is not used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, including generalized or third-party models.

Use only to provide add-on features

Google API data is used solely to provide Anviva DocGen features you request: generating documents from Google Sheets™ and Google Docs™ templates, saving a template, uploading images in the add-on, licensing, and responding to support requests. Details: Privacy Policy.
